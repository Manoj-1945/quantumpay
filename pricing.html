Frequently Asked Questions

How does the hybrid pricing model work?
You pay a fixed monthly base fee for access to the quantum key pool and cryptographic engine, plus a small per-transaction fee based on your volume. If you exceed plan limits, overage is billed at the plan's per-transaction rate.
What happens when a quantum token is generated?
Your server sends transaction parameters to our API. We derive true quantum entropy, encrypt via Kyber-1024 (NIST FIPS 203 Level 5), sign via Dilithium-3, write to the immutable Merkle ledger, and dispatch a signed HMAC webhook to your endpoint in under 200ms.
Are there setup fees or long-term contracts?
No setup fees for Starter or Professional tiers. Subscriptions are billed month-to-month with no lock-in. Enterprise contracts offer annual discounts with custom SLAs.
